    §3 experiments Fig. 1–3 raw data on GitHub the first one changed how I delegate

    Measured, then written up.

    Questions that came up while building Yep and C3, answered with a benchmark. Each one says what we changed because of it.

    Can the small model do the chores?

    Method
    74 bounded tasks with a stated rule, across 10 categories, scored for Haiku and Sonnet.
    Result
    Haiku 0.996, Sonnet 1.000, at about 1/17 of the cost.
    Change
    I hand that kind of task to Haiku by default now.
    RESULTS.md →

    And when the chores get harder?

    Method
    60 harder items in 6 categories, scored for Haiku, Sonnet and Opus.
    Result
    Haiku 0.968, Sonnet 0.994, Opus 1.000. Haiku at about 1/16 of Sonnet's cost.
    Change
    Haiku for 5 of the 6 categories. Judgment calls with no stated rule get a check.
    RESULTS.md →

    Do shorter replies lose anything?

    Method
    The same prompts to Opus with and without ConciseClaude's answer-first rules.
    Result
    Replies 67% shorter and 24% cheaper, with safety steps kept in full.
    Change
    It is the reply style in my Claude Code sessions.
