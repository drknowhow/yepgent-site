    §6.1 scientist tools 1 tool one page, one job

    Tools

    Each tool is one page that does one job. The images and data you open are processed in this browser and never uploaded.

    §6.2 method

    How they are built

    • LocalAnalysis runs in your browser. Nothing you open is sent to a server.
    • MeasuredEvery number a tool reports is measured from your pixels by fixed, tested code. No language model produces a measurement.
    • CheckedBefore a tool goes up here it is scored against images whose answers are known, and it has to meet its targets.
    • FlaggedResults carry quality flags. Extrapolated or doubtful values are marked, not hidden.
    • ReproducibleA saved session keeps your settings and edits, so a result can be opened and checked again later.
